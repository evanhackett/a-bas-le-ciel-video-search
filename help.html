Search help

This site searches the full text of every video on the channel, including the transcripts. There are three main options to decide how your words are matched: exact, any, and all.

For example, searching "afghanistan nazi" will show different results depending on the mode:

Mode Matches a video when… How many videos match
exact the phrase "afghanistan nazi" appears verbatim zero results
any either "afghanistan" or "nazi" appears 441 results
all both "afghanistan" and "nazi" appear somewhere 59 results

Each mode can be useful depending on what you are looking for.

Worth knowing

  • The Title, Description and Transcript checkboxes control where to look.
  • Matching is by substring, not whole words — "cat" also matches "catastrophe". This can be useful when you aren't sure which variant of a word was used. For example, searching "nihil" matches on "nihilist" and "nihilism", etc.
  • An empty search box shows everything.
  • Searches need at least three characters, and in any mode so does each individual word. This is to get around performance issues. Besides, two-letter and one-letter phrases match nearly everything.
  • Capitalisation is ignored.
  • Your search terms are highlighted in the results. This may require scrolling carefully through the transcript to find the highlighted text.
  • Everything runs in your browser: the entire archive downloads once when the page loads, which is why the first load takes a moment.
