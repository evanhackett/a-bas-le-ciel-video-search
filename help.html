Search help

This site searches the full text of every video on the channel, including the transcripts. The three options above the search box decide how your words are matched.

The same search, antinatalism ecology, run three ways:

Mode Matches a video when… Roughly how many
exact the phrase "antinatalism ecology" appears verbatim none — nobody says it that way
all both "antinatalism" and "ecology" appear somewhere a handful
any either "antinatalism" or "ecology" appears hundreds

If a search returns nothing, widen the mode: exact → all → any.

Worth knowing

  • The Title, Description and Transcript checkboxes control where to look. Transcripts are much the largest, so turning that one off narrows things a lot.
  • Matching is by substring, not whole words — cat also matches "catastrophe".
  • An empty search box shows everything, newest first.
  • Searches need at least three characters, and in any mode so does each individual word.
  • Capitalisation is ignored, and your terms are highlighted in the results.
  • Everything runs in your browser: the archive downloads once when the page loads, which is why the first load takes a moment.
