Search help

This site searches the full text of every video on the channel — including the transcripts, which is the part YouTube's own search will not do for you. The three options at the top of the search box decide how your words are matched.

The three modes

Contains exact search phrase

Finds your words together, in the order you typed them. Searching the difference between matches only videos where those three words appear side by side, exactly like that.

Use it when you remember the actual wording — a turn of phrase, a name, a quote you want to find again. It is the narrowest option, and the one most likely to return nothing if your memory of the phrasing is slightly off.

Contains all words in search phrase

Finds videos containing every one of your words, anywhere, in any order. Searching antinatalism ecology matches a video that mentions antinatalism in the title and ecology halfway through the transcript — the words never have to appear near each other.

Use it when you are looking for a topic rather than a phrase, and you want every word to count. This is usually the most useful of the three for exploring.

Contains any word in search phrase

Finds videos containing at least one of your words. Searching antinatalism ecology matches everything mentioning either term, including videos with nothing to do with the other.

Use it when you are unsure which word was used and want to cast a wide net — for instance searching vegan vegetarian to catch either. Expect a lot of results.

Side by side

The same search, antinatalism ecology, run three ways over the current archive:

Mode Matches a video when… Roughly how many
exact the phrase "antinatalism ecology" appears verbatim none — nobody says it that way
all both "antinatalism" and "ecology" appear somewhere a handful
any either "antinatalism" or "ecology" appears hundreds

If a search returns nothing, widening the mode is usually the fix: exact → all → any, in that order.

Where it searches

The three checkboxes — Title, Description and Transcript — control which parts of each video are looked at. All three are on by default.

Transcripts are by far the largest of the three, so turning that one off narrows things dramatically. It is worth doing when a common word is drowning you in results and you only care about videos whose title is about the subject.

Worth knowing

  • Matching is by substring, not whole words. Searching cat also matches "catastrophe" and "education". This is often useful — natal catches both "antinatalism" and "natalist" — but it does mean short words match a great deal.
  • Searches need at least three characters. In any mode every individual word must also be at least three characters, because a two-letter word matched on its own would return nearly the whole archive. All and exact modes allow short words, since there they only narrow the results — so war of the worlds is a fine all-words search.
  • Capitalisation is ignored everywhere.
  • Your search terms are highlighted in the results. In exact mode the whole phrase is highlighted; in the other two, each word is highlighted wherever it appears.
  • Everything happens in your browser. The whole archive is downloaded once when the page loads — which is why the first load takes a moment — and every search after that runs locally, with nothing sent anywhere.
